Forum Philosophy

No One Evolves Alone.

Learn Together · Think Together · Evolve Together

The Japan–Korea International Mental Coaching Forum is an academic and practical exchange platform where coaches, practitioners, and leaders bridge experiences, research, and cultural insights.

As artificial intelligence and rapid technological advances reshape human society, the essence of mental coaching—listening deeply, unlocking authentic potential, and guiding self-determined action—has never been more vital. Through open dialogue and mutual respect, we lay the groundwork for the next generation of coaching.

📘

Learn Together

Bridging elite athletic coaching methodologies with academic sports psychology and clinical insights.

💡

Think Together

Reflecting on human dignity, genuine empowerment, and the vital role of coaches alongside generative AI.

🌱

Evolve Together

Cultivating an enduring bilateral partnership and professional community across borders.

🎉 Double 10th Anniversary Milestone (2016–2026)

Celebrating 10 Years of MCCI & Field Flow

In 2026, both Korea's MCCI (Mental Coaching Center Int'l) and Japan's Field Flow celebrate their 10th anniversaries. For a decade, both institutions have pioneered human-centered mental coaching and performance excellence across sports, education, and business in Korea and Japan.

🇰🇷 Korea · Est. 2016

MCCI (Mental Coaching Center Int'l)

Mental Coaching Center Int'l (MCCI) has pioneered international-standard mental coaching in Korea for 10 years, fostering authentic human agency, values-driven self-determination, and peak psychological performance for Olympic athletes, leaders, and educators.

📍 3F, J-Add Bldg., 1, Yangjaecheon-ro 15-gil, Seocho-gu, Seoul
👤 Coach Vicky Cheon (VP) · 010-4457-3850
🇯🇵 Japan · Est. 2016

Field Flow Japan

Pioneering the coaching of invisible field dynamics and team flow states in Japanese sports and organizational leadership. Creating environments where collective intuition and deep empathy drive peak achievements.

📍 Tokyo, Japan
👤 Motonari Takahashi (Executive Coach)
